At the end of *La La Land*, Mia walks into that jazz club and sees the sign she once drew for Sebastian — **Seb's** — glowing in neon. The whole story lights up with it.

That's the feeling I wanted for my personal site:

- The night is deep blue, not pure black;
- The lights are warm, the letters glow;
- The music is quiet, but always on.

## What you'll find here

Essays, works, and the software I've built. Think of it as my little club — push the door open anytime.

```ts
const club = {
  name: "Seb's",
  openAt: "midnight",
  music: "jazz",
};
```

Welcome in.
