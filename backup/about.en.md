Hi, I'm fhf — a front-end developer, nine years of code so far.

I set out from Hejin in Shanxi, spent a few years in Beijing and a spell in Qingdao, and now live in Shanghai. The day job is turning requirements into interfaces people can use; the evenings are for small software of my own — most of it written for myself first, and named only later.

Outside of work there is a lot I love. I have played the violin since I was a child and still do; music, film and games are everyday things — Dota 2, Overwatch, It Takes Two, basketball and table tennis, long table-top nights of Werewolf and Sanguosha, and trips taken on short notice. I have never stopped being curious about the world.

This site is my small study: thoughts go into the writing, the software sits on the shelf, and the lab holds whatever is still growing. One light stays on — come in any time.
